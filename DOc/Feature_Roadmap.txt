# RunClub Feature Roadmap 🚀

## 1. Classes / RPG Elements 🛡️⚡
Allow players to choose a "Role" / "Class" that grants passive bonuses.
- **The Sprinter**: Claims land 2x faster (fewer points needed for loop), but trails disappear faster.
- **The Tank**: Territories require 2 passes to be stolen (extra durability).
- **The Spy**: Invisible on the map (no trail), but cannot claim land (only steal/sabotage).
- **The Scout**: Larger vision radius (sees enemies from further away).

## 2. Defensive Power-ups 💎
Players earn points/currency from holding territory, which can be spent on active items.
- **Shield (Cost: 500pts)**: Drop a shield on your territory. It becomes "Locked" for 10 minutes (Cannot be stolen by enemies).
- **Mine / Trap (Cost: 300pts)**: Drop an invisible trap. If an enemy runs over it, their screen glitches, they lose 50% of their current path, or are temporarily frozen.
- **Decoy (Cost: 100pts)**: Spawns a fake runner on the map to confuse enemies.

## 3. "King of the Hill" Zones 👑
Designate special static areas on the map (parks, landmarks) as High Value Zones.
- **Global Hotspots**: Pre-defined zones by the admin.
- **Scoring**: 
    - Normal Land: 1 point / m²
    - Hotspot Land: 10 points / m²
- **Objective**: Forces teams to converge and fight over specific areas rather than spreading out indefinitely.

## 4. Anti-Cheat & Security 👮‍♂️
Prevent GPS spoofing and driving.
- **Speed Limit**: If speed > 30km/h (approx 8.3m/s), pause recording. Warning: "You are moving too fast! No driving allowed."
- **Teleport Check**: If distance between two updates is impossibly large for the time delta, ignore the point.
- **Device Attestation**: Use App Check to ensure request comes from the real app (prevent script bots).

## 5. Progression System 📈
- **Level Up**: XP based on area claimed.
- **Badges**: "Marathoner" (Run 42km total), "Conqueror" (Steal 100 lands), "Defender" (Hold a spot for 24h).
- **Leaderboards**: Weekly/Monthly resets to keep it fresh.
